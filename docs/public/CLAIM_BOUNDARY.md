# Claim boundary

Allowed claims:

- controlled Abelian logical phase primitive;
- numerical convergence in trap depth and integration step;
- low leakage and near-unit singular values in the registered protocol;
- explicit commutation of the current gate family.

Excluded claims:

- non-Abelian anyons or braiding;
- universal quantum computation;
- fault tolerance or a topological quantum computer;
- a microscopic experimental implementation.
