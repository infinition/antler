# Public archive scope

This repository publishes the complete Phase 0 to 4.7 evidence chain for the
Abelian digital phase primitive. It includes construction checks, logical
subspace reconstruction, transport controls, convergence data, path
deformations and repeated-gate composition.

It also publishes a separately curated Phase 5 to 8 research archive. That
archive contains constrained non-Abelian searches, negative controls, scoped
no-go results and explicit extension requirements. Its evidence and claim
boundary are summarized in `PHASE5_8_RESEARCH_ARCHIVE.md`.

The separation prevents the paper on the validated Abelian primitive from
being mistaken for a claim of non-Abelian braiding or topological quantum
computation. The Phase 5 to 8 material is an archival research record, not a
second completed non-Abelian paper.
